Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use codebitee (codebitee.github.io) and how it is used. It covers the website, its tools, its blog articles, and any advertising or paid features that are added to it.

1. Information we collect

You can use the site without creating an account, and we do not ask for your name or email just to browse it. Information may still be collected in these ways:

  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us by email or through a social account, we receive the details you choose to share and use them only to reply.
  • Tool inputs. Where a tool works entirely in your browser, your input stays on your device. If a tool ever sends data to a server, that tool’s page says so.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

At the moment the site itself does not set cookies to track you. This can change when advertising, analytics or paid features are added, and this page will be updated when that happens. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may then not work as expected.

3. Advertising

This site may show ads, now or in the future, including ads served by Google AdSense and other advertising partners. If it does:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site or other sites.
  • Google’s advertising cookies, such as the DoubleClick cookie, let Google and its partners serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we will ask for your consent through a cookie notice before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used.

4. Analytics

We may use an analytics service to understand which pages and tools are used, so we can improve them. Such services may use cookies or similar technologies and receive data like your approximate location, device and pages visited. If analytics are added, the service used will be named here.

5. Paid tools and payments

Some tools or features may be paid. Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider, and we do not see or store your full card or bank details. The provider’s own privacy policy applies to the payment information you give them. We may keep basic records of a purchase, such as the date, amount and the email used, to provide the service and meet legal obligations.

6. Links to other websites

The site links to other websites, including other projects and services, and some of them open in a new tab. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

7. How we use information

  • to run, maintain and improve the site and its tools
  • to reply to messages you send us
  • to show ads and measure how the site is used, where those are enabled
  • to keep the site secure and to meet legal requirements

We do not sell your personal information for money.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act may give you rights over your personal data. These can include the right to access it, correct it, delete it, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy, use the details in the Contact section of the homepage, or reach us on GitHub.
